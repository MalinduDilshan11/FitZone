Fitness Tips

Set Realistic Goals

Start with achievable goals to avoid burnout. Setting attainable goals keeps motivation high.

Stay Hydrated

Drinking enough water is essential. It helps with recovery, keeps energy up, and flushes toxins.

Warm Up Properly

A proper warm-up prevents injuries and preps your muscles. Start with light cardio and dynamic stretches.

Fuel Up Right

Eat balanced meals with protein, carbs, and healthy fats. Choose whole foods for optimal performance.

Prioritize Sleep

Aim for 7-9 hours nightly to boost recovery, focus, and energy. Quality rest is vital for performance.
